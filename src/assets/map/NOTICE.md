# Map data attribution

Generated by `scripts/generate-map.py` -- do not edit.

Country outlines come from
[Natural Earth](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson)
(110m admin-0), released into the **public domain**. Natural Earth asks for no
permission and no attribution, but credits its contributors at
<https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/>.

Coordinates are pre-projected to Web Mercator in a 1000x1000 space, latitude
clamped to +/-85.05 degrees.
